GCList: Garbage Collection in Concurrent Sets

M.Tech Research Project  •  Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad

Designed and evaluated GCList, a concurrent-set representation with application-specific memory reclamation and node reuse. The work explores both lock-based and lock-free implementations for concurrent linked sets.

The research addresses memory reclamation challenges in concurrent data structures and evaluates GCList against approaches including LazyList, Harris' LockFreeList, Shared Pointers and Hazard Pointers.
